# Wooden fence

| | |
|---|---|
| SKU | `buy-fence` (Build tab `building`) |
| price | `SKUS['buy-fence'].price` |
| size | 1 × 1, on untilled ground |
| unlocked by | `unlock-landscaping` |
| demolish | yes |

A fence piece in the middle of a tile that joins the fences on the four tiles beside it. A closed ring of fences forms a fenced area ([[features/fences]]).

## Use

Placed on untilled ground without a burrow, or on the tile of a crop sensor or Pressure plate (`isFenceSite`); elsewhere **Fences need untilled ground**. Dragging lays a run. The Fence tool stays selected after placing. A tile that already has one is refused (**Already fenced**). A crop sensor or Pressure plate placed on a fence tile watches the inside of the fenced area ([[items/buildings/sensors-crop]], [[items/buildings/sensors-environment]]).

## Art

Drawn from fence arms (`fenceArms`).
