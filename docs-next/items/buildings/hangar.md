Where vehicles and trailers are bought and stored ([[features/vehicles]], [[items/other/vehicles]]).

## Use

Clicking it walks the gardener to it and opens its panel: buy a Quad or Tractor and trailers, take a vehicle out onto the field, send it out on a route. A vehicle parked in the hangar is stored and does not move.

## Connections

No chest input or output.
