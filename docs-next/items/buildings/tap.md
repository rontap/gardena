A bucket filling point on a water network, so the gardener does not walk back to the source.

## Use

**Fill** with a [[items/other/bucket]] at `TAP_RATE` L/s while the network's tanks hold water; when they are empty, only as fast as the sources refill. It joins a network at any corner of its tile and has no tank of its own ([[features/water]]).
