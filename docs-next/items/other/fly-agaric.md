A red spotted mushroom that comes up on untilled ground around a tree. It does not lose freshness. The Infuser uses it to make Infused Extract ([[items/buildings/infuser]]). The Compost box takes it at `COMPOST_VALUE['fly-agaric']` and the Furnace at `FURNACE_VALUE['fly-agaric']` ([[features/machines]]). A Necronomicon page asks for it ([[features/necronomicon]]).
