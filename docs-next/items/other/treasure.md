# Treasure

| | |
|---|---|
| item kind | `'treasure'` |
| unit | coins |
| obtained from | a Common or Uncommon burrow ([[features/burrow]]) |
| sold at | — |

Money dug out of a burrow. It lies on the ground; **Pick up** adds its coins to the farm's money at once and removes it. It never goes into a hand or inventory.

The amount is a whole number from the entry's range, each equally likely, drawn at the dig: `BURROW_TREASURE_MIN` to `BURROW_TREASURE_MAX` coins from a Common burrow, `BURROW_TREASURE_UNCOMMON_MIN` to `BURROW_TREASURE_UNCOMMON_MAX` from an Uncommon one ([[features/burrow]]).
