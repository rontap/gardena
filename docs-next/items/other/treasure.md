# Treasure

| | |
|---|---|
| item kind | `'treasure'` |
| unit | coins |
| obtained from | burrow loot |
| sold at | — |

Money dug out of a burrow. It lies on the ground; **Pick up** adds its coins to the farm's money at once and removes it. It never goes into a hand or inventory.

The amount is (`TREASURE_COINS_BASE` + a roll up to `TREASURE_COINS_SPAN`) × the burrow's loot roll ([[features/burrow]]).
