The seed of one tree: species, variety and quality. Name: **{Name} seed**.

## Use

**Plant {Name}** on a soft untilled tile whose upper neighbour is also soft untilled (bare or grass). The tree covers both tiles and starts its juvenile period with the seed's variety ([[features/trees]]). Crops: [[items/crops/apple]], [[items/crops/apricot]], [[items/crops/olive]], [[items/crops/cherry]].
