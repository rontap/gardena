# 2.11.0: Market rework

Draft. Current contract generation, then the exclusive `GoodGroup` cut. Generation as it runs: [[features/contracts]]. Version digits: [[process/versions]].

Assumption: `NamedProduce` is every Named or Heirloom variety whose `purpose` is produce (Fresh). Bread and mill sugar leave `CONTRACT_GOODS`. **Any jam** / **Any spirit** are not in this cut.

## State now

`rollBoard(rng, day, slots, repDay, penaltyRate)` builds today's contracts from the world seed, the day number, position count, reputation copied at the previous day's end, and `hard.penaltyRate`. It reads no planted crop, no owned SKU, no research.

Positions: `CONTRACT_OFFERS` (6) + **Broker** rank, cap `BROKER_MAX_TIER` (3). Ids: `day × CONTRACT_SLOT_MAX + slot` with `CONTRACT_SLOT_MAX` 9. Two of positions 0–5 pay a prize (`PRIZE_SLOTS`). Broker positions pay money.

### Mix budget and pairs

`opened = MIX_FLOOR (2) + D × MIX_SHARE (0.5) − DEADLINE_COST[kind]`, floor `−BUDGET_OVERDRAFT` (−3). Pair when `opened ≥ PAIR_COST` (10); each line then gets `(budget − 10) / 2`.

Two lines from about: long D ≥ 8, normal D ≥ 16, tight D ≥ 32.

`sameFamily`: two jams, two spirits, or the same `StallGoodId`. Wine and cider are casks, not spirits.

### Money target and pay

`load(D) = LOAD_MIN (0.12) + (LOAD_MAX − LOAD_MIN) (1.23) × ((D + LOAD_D_OFFSET 6) / (60 + 6)) ^ LOAD_CURVE (2)`.

`solo = REFERENCE_GOLD_PER_DAY × days × load(D)`. `REFERENCE_GOLD_PER_DAY` is the median over `CONTRACT_GOODS` of `unitOf(good) × FEASIBLE_PER_DAY[good]`.

```
clean   = round(Σ amount × cleanUnit)
markup  = round(MARKUP_BASE 0.15 + MARKUP_PER_DIFFICULTY 0.004 × eff + MARKUP_BAND[kind], hundredths)
reward  = round(clean × (1 + markup))
penalty = round(penaltyRate × clean)
```

`penaltyRate`: Peaceful 0.1, Normal 0.2, Hard 0.4.

Amount: largest of `NICE_AMOUNTS` `[2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40, 50, 60, 80, 100]` at or below `min(target / cleanUnit, FEASIBLE_PER_DAY × days × scale(day))`. `scale = min(1, SCALE_START 0.35 + day / SCALE_DAYS 24)`. Floor `AMOUNT_MIN` 2.

Early-day scale is below 1 until day 16.

## Change plans

A contract good is an `Item` with `kind` and `variety` pinned (`Extract<Item, { kind; crop; variety }>`). Default variety `Plain` (`VARIETIES[crop][0]`, `'base'`). `Alcohol<C>` / `Jam<C>` follow `SPIRIT_OF` / jam `crop` of that fruit. `Demand` carries that slice plus `amount`. Groups are exclusive member unions. `Produce = BaseProduce | MidProduce | AdvProduce`. `GoodGroup` is the union of every subtype. `goodContractGroup` is dropped. `get(BaseProduce)` returns every `BaseProduce` member.

Flour and olive oil sit in `Processed`. `SpecialtyProcessed` adds Arbequina oil.

### Subtypes

```
BaseProduce        = Carrot | Potato | Wheat
MidProduce         = Tomato | Apple | Apricot
AdvProduce         = Cherry | Olive | Grape | Raspberry
NamedProduce       = Tomato<GreenZebra> | Apple<PinkLady> | Apricot<Blenheim> | Cherry<Bing>
Produce            = BaseProduce | MidProduce | AdvProduce

Alcohol            = Alcohol<Potato> | Alcohol<Apricot> | Alcohol<Grape> | Alcohol<Apple> | Alcohol<_Mixed>
SpecialtyAlcohol   = Alcohol<Apple<KingstonBlack>> | Alcohol<Apricot<Klosterneuburger>> | Alcohol<Grape<Keknyelu>>

Processed          = Jam<Cherry> | Jam<Raspberry> | Jam<Tomato> | Jam<Apricot> | Jam<Grape> | Flour | Oil
SpecialtyProcessed = Jam<Raspberry<BlackRaspberry>> | Jam<Tomato<SanMarzano>> | Jam<Grape<Concord>> | Oil<Arbequina>

Utility            = Vanilla | SugarCane | Chilli

GoodGroup          = Produce | NamedProduce | Alcohol | SpecialtyAlcohol | Processed | SpecialtyProcessed | Utility
```

`NamedProduce` only where `purpose` is produce. Alcohol is still or barrel of that crop: Potato vodka, Apricot brandy, Grape wine, Apple cider, `_Mixed` mixed spirit.

### Type → data

Types erase. Same PascalCase name in both namespaces: `const BaseProduce` is the member list, `type BaseProduce` is the union. `get(xs)` returns `xs`. `get(BaseProduce)` is every `BaseProduce` member.

### Out of `CONTRACT_GOODS` under this cut

Bread, mill sugar. Bintje vodka stays off `SpecialtyAlcohol`.

### Generation (not yet numbered)

Pick a subtype, then a member from `get` of that subtype. Default `<Plain>` unless the subtype is `NamedProduce` / `SpecialtyAlcohol` / `SpecialtyProcessed`.

`GOOD_TIER` / `GOOD_COST` / `FEASIBLE_PER_DAY` retune per subtype or per member — values not in this draft.

Prizes, companies, reputation, deadline kinds, load curve: unchanged until a later cut.
