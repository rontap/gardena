## Summary

`footOutline` builds a directed-edge set, drops reverse pairs, then walks, with a 1-unit pad around the 2-wide stroke. That is [[architecture/view]] `view.outline`: one `data-cell-stroke` / `data-furnace-cover` path, internal edges gone. House 4×3, still 2×1, and a full 7×8 covering each yield one `M`. The walk used to stall with a defensive `break` and emit another `M` from leftover edges; it now consumes outgoing edges and throws if a vertex has none.

- Description: After reverse cancel, the walk does `if (opts === undefined) break` / `if (nxt === undefined) break` and still emits `Z`.
