# Trees

`prop-{apple,apricot,olive,cherry}-tree.svg` are 24×48 vertical two-tile props with `grow` `unripe` `ripe` groups. Fruit icons `fruit-{apple,apricot,olive,cherry}.svg` are 24×24 with `common` `rare` `heirloom`; uncommon uses common. Pink Lady is the apple `heirloom` group.

`grow` is the **sapling** in the ground: a species-shaped mini crown on a whip trunk over a fresh soil mound, drawn low so it reads small. Not an item face.

`unripe` / `ripe` are the mature tree. Ink silhouette, `#3a6232` canopy, `#4a7c3f` mid, `#6bc04a` highlight top-left; trunk ink + `#6b4423` with a lit `#8a5a32` left edge and a root flare at `y ≈ 44`. Silhouettes are the species tell: apple round and tall, apricot wide and low with forked branches, olive narrow and gnarled with `#cfc6b0` silver flecks, cherry twin-lobed and slim. `ripe` adds outlined 4×4 fruit (cherry 3×3) over `unripe` — the delta is the fruit, never a canopy tint.

Tree seed items are `item-seed-{apple,apricot,olive,cherry}.svg`, 24×24, no groups. Each is the fruit **cut in half**, filling the icon like a `fruit-*` face: species skin ring, flesh, and a pit or pips as the focus. Skins are the tell — apple `#c43c3c`, apricot `#d4a017`, olive `#6b4423`, cherry `#8b3a2a` with the long stalk.
