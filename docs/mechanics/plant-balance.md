# Plant balance

Weed appearance chance is `0.035` per fallow roll, down 30% from `0.05`.

Plant and weed fertilizer consumption are each multiplied by `0.6`, reducing global fertilizer use by 40%.

Plant happiness is a live quality state. Good water and fertilizer raise it; bad water or fertilizer lowers it. At ripening it modifies the chance of moving the plant's rarity up or down. It does not directly increase growth speed.
