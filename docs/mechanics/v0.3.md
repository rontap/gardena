# v0.3 mechanics

Supersedes [[mechanics/v0.1]] where this file names a replacement. Types: [[architecture/v0.3]].

v0.1 world, pipes, freshness, rarity, market, research stay except below.

Gone: `Plant.thirst`, `Plant.thirsty`, `Plant.critical`, `PLANT_THIRST`, per-coordinate soil RNG (`hash(seed, 'soil', …)`).

Forbidden: water on a plant; water on `untilled` or `infertile`; fertilizer affecting growth, happiness, sale, or rarity; a plot losing its soil on harvest, dig, or death.

## Soil

Water and fertilizer belong to the **dirt**, not the plant. One `Soil` per tilled plot, carried through every state change of that plot.

```
water       0..1 L    SOIL_WATER_MAX = 1
fertilizer  0..1      FERT_PLOT_MAX = 1
```

Tilling makes the soil: `water = SOIL_TILL_WATER = 0.75`, `fertilizer = goodness(seed, col, row)`.

`untilled` and `infertile` hold no soil. Mining very-hard → `infertile`, still no soil.

Soil survives: plant → ripe → harvest → empty, plant death, rot, weeding. Digging a plant leaves the soil it grew in. Only tilling fresh ground, or clearing a deleted building, mints a new `Soil`.

`WITHER = 0.33`, `CRITICAL = 0.1`, `HEALTH = 0.5` now read `soil.water`.

## Ground from noise

Ground kind is derived from the same `[0,1]` goodness field that sets fertilizer, not from an independent roll.

```
goodness < 0.20 → very-hard
goodness < 0.34 → hard
else              soft
```

Hard and very-hard therefore always till into poor dirt. That is the whole difference between them and soft.

Field: 3-octave value noise, frequencies `1/9`, `1/4.5`, `1/2.2`, amplitudes `0.5`, `0.3`, `0.2`; contrast `0.5 + (raw − 0.5) × 1.9`; plus a base boost, clamped to `[0,1]`.

Base boost, centred on `DOOR`, linear taper to `+0` at `r = 8`:

```
fade  = r >= 8 ? 0 : 1 − r / 8
boost = fade × (0.4 + 0.35 × hash(seed, 'soil-boost', col, row))
```

Typical chunk: ~75% soft, ~15% hard, ~10% very-hard. Rock placement keeps its own roll. `clearBase` still forces soft ground inside `r = 8`; it does not touch goodness, so a start can be soft but only mediocre.

## Watering

Bucket pours `WATER_POUR = 1` L into the soil, clamped at 1 L. Costs 1 L of bucket.

Waterable: `empty`, `weed`, `growing`, `ripe` — and only while `soil.water < 1`. Full soil prompts **Soil is full**. Bare tilled soil is waterable; that is the point.

Planting sets no water. A seed starts on whatever the dirt already holds.

Sprinklers still only target `growing`, and now add to `soil.water`.

## Fertilizer

Fertilizer bag: 5 L, $6, `unlock-fertilizer` ($9 / 40s, plants tree).

One application tops the plot to full and spends only the gap: `use = min(1 − soil.fertilizer, bag.liters)`. A plot at 70% costs 0.3 L. An emptied bag leaves the hand.

Growing plants draw `PLANT_FERT_PER_SEC = 1/720` — a full plot in 3 days. Apple trees and shrubs draw nothing.

Fertilizer changes nothing else yet. It is stored, spent, and displayed only.

## Weed

New plot kind. Two variants. Takes tilled soil that holds no seed.

Roll once per big tick per `empty` plot: `WEED_CHANCE = 0.05`. Owned by the state update — no per-cell timer.

Grows in `WEED_GROW = 60`s (`sprout` under 0.4 maturity, then `grow`). Drinks `WEED_WATER_PER_SEC = 0.008` L/s and `WEED_FERT_PER_SEC = 1/240` — a full plot of each in ~125s and ~240s.

Shovel → `empty`, same soil, 1 use. Prompt **Pull weed**. Cannot be planted on.

## Grass

Cosmetic cover on `untilled`. Three variants.

One roll per big tick for the whole world: `GRASS_CHANCE = 0.5`, then up to 24 sampled cells to find a spot. Owned by the state update.

Eligible: `untilled`, ground not very-hard, cover bare, no drop on the cell. Appears fully grown. Tilling removes it. Roughly 10 tufts a day.

## Big tick

`BIG_TICK = 10`s, accumulated on `World`. Drives weed and grass spawning only. `World.bigTicks` counts them and salts both rolls.

## Named invariants (tests after impl)

Keep v0.1 invariants except those reading `Plant.thirst`.

1. Tilling untilled soil yields `empty` with `water === 0.75` and `fertilizer === goodness(seed, col, row)`.
2. Planting does not change `soil.water`; the new `growing` plot holds the same `Soil` instance.
3. Harvest yields `empty` holding the same `Soil` instance, water unchanged.
4. Bucket on `empty` soil at 0.2 L → 1 L, bucket down exactly 1 L.
5. Fertilizer bag on a plot at 0.7 → plot 1.0, bag down exactly 0.3 L.
6. A weed lowers both water and fertilizer of its plot over time.
7. Shovel on `weed` → `empty`, same `Soil`, no drop.
8. Grass never appears on very-hard ground, tilled soil, or a cell with a drop.
9. Sprinkler AoE raises `soil.water` of `growing` plots and nothing else.
10. `Plant` has no `thirst`.
