# Legible Build, Living World

Spec. Two halves. Build modes the player can see, and a world with motion above the cell.

Graduates into [[ui/place]], [[ui/lens]], [[ui/sensors]], [[ui/docks]], [[ui/hud]], [[mechanics/water]], [[mechanics/sensors]], [[items/irrigation]], [[art/vfx]], [[art/actor]], [[art/sensors]], [[art/tilled-edges]], [[architecture/view]]. Types: `sim/pipe.ts` `sim/log.ts` `view/hit.ts` `view/vfx.ts`. Dump identity [[GLOBAL_VERSION]]. No migrate.

Diagnosis: arming, the edge lattice, and the lens are all invisible, and the lens a build needs is the one closing the dock takes away. VFX is per-cell state paint, so no system reads as a system.

## Lattice

`overlay`. While a `PIPE_PLACE` sku or delete is armed, paint every owned edge that carries no segment and no well. Stroke `ink`, `LATTICE_ALPHA` — preference. Viewport-culled, `eventMode` `'none'`.

Pipes go on the lines. The player sees that before the first click, not after the first snap.

## Pipe run

Left-drag is the run only when the press lands on an edge. Press within `EDGE_HIT` of the nearest edge → pending run. Press anywhere else in the tile → pan, exactly as unarmed. No rebound button, no modifier.

`routeEdges(a: Vertex, b: Vertex): Edge[]` — pure, `hit.ts`. L path along the lattice, long axis first. Shift flips the corner. `pendingPipe = routeEdges(anchor, hoverVertex).filter(pipeOk)`, recomputed every move. Not accumulated — a fast diagonal drag is a connected run, not confetti.

Anchor is the vertex nearest the press.

| pointer | result |
|---|---|
| up, pending non-empty | commit `placePipe` per edge, log order. Anchor drops. Stay armed |
| up within `EDGE_HIT` of the anchor, pending empty | keep the anchor. Click-click is live |
| click with an anchor | commit that leg, re-anchor at its far vertex. Chains |
| right-click / Esc / Cancel | drop anchor and preview, nothing paid |

Chip while a run is pending: segment count and total. Total over `money` → whole run blocked, **Cannot afford**, no partial commit. Mid-run truncation is gone.

Junction ghosts and ghost wetness unchanged — [[ui/place]]. Add: any sprinkler the run would newly wet paints its `aoe()` wash while the run is pending. The consequence is visible before paying.

## Valve

`Gate = { kind: 'bare' } | { kind: 'valve'; open: boolean }`. `{ kind: 'smart' }` is gone.

`buy-smart-valve` is deleted. `unlock-smart-irrigation` grants, on one row: every vertex sprinkler gains a signal `in` and the crop dial, **and every valve gains a signal `in`**. The same rule the sprinkler already had. No SKU, no second object, no rebuilding valves the player already owns.

| valve | conducts | click |
|---|---|---|
| unwired | `open` | toggles, walk-to, as today |
| wired | held input `1` | no-op. Look **Valve — wired** |

`open` is kept underneath a wired valve and rules again the moment the last wire on that `in` is deleted. Unwired is **not** closed.

`smartHold` → `valveHold`, keyed by edge, entries only for wired valves. `SENSOR_HOLD` on that input, unchanged.

Auto-lay: `buy-valve` on an owned edge with no pipe and no well places pipe **and** valve, charging both. Both or neither. **Valve needs a pipe** is deleted.

Delete valve: pipe stays, incident wires drop. Delete pipe under a wired valve: both go, wires drop.

Guest: valve place and click stay host-only, the manual rule. Guest may wire one.

Art: `pipe-valve.svg` groups `open` / `closed` unchanged. A valve that owns a port draws `pipe-valve-jack.svg` group `jack` over the edge midpoint. `pipe-smart-valve.svg` and `item-smart-valve.svg` retire. — [[art/sensors]]

## Lens lock

A lens is either a peek or a job. Today every lens is a job and the player never asked for one.

View-local, `map.tsx`. Not `World`, not Save, not logged.

| state | is |
|---|---|
| `lens` | the picked lens |
| `lensLock` | survives the dock closing |
| `toolLens` | forced by an armed sku, lives exactly as long as that arming |

Effective lens = `toolLens` when set, else `lens`. Disarm restores the picked lens; it never overwrites it.

`toolLens` is `pipes` while a `PIPE_PLACE` sku or delete is armed, `sensors` while a sensor-cell sku is armed.

Dock: **No lens** button, then a **Lock view** `Btn`, `selected` on `lensLock`, then the cards. Lock while `lens === 'off'` is disabled and the callout says why — [[ui/callout-hover]].

Close the dock (**×**, rail toggle, Esc): `lensLock` → the lens stays. Else `lens = 'off'`. **No lens** clears both.

Rail **Lens** sub-line carries the lens id; locked adds a lock glyph and an **×** hit that clears lens and lock without opening the dock. A locked lens is always one click from off, from anywhere.

`leaveShop` no longer touches the lens. Delete that rule from [[ui/place]], [[ui/docks]], [[ui/lens]] — `toolLens` already ends with the arming.

Two paths state the long intent, so both lock:

- Build shelf `logic` → `lens = 'sensors'`, `lensLock = true`. Still arms nothing.
- Confirming a sensor-cell place → `lens = 'sensors'`, `lensLock = true`. Look **Sensors lens locked**, once per arming.

That is the build → exit → lens → wire round trip, closed: the sensor just placed is still wireable when the dock shuts.

## Ports

The paint is the hitbox. `PORT_HIT` — preference. A port is a disc of `PORT_HIT` at `portXY`, live only when the effective lens is `sensors`. Port chrome draws at that radius.

Cell body is the device action in **every** lens: Flip, Press, Tune. The lens-dependent lever hitbox is deleted, and so is the half-cell port. AND / OR get three discs, not left and right halves.

Output-only devices with no body action — water, fert, harvest, day, water-system, vehicle-detector, chest, freezer, seed-silo, additive-store — keep whole-cell = `out`, `sensors` only.

Sprinkler `in`: disc at the vertex. Valve `in`: disc at the edge midpoint.

## Wire

Drag as well as click-click, one path. Press on an `out` disc → `armWire`. Release on an `in` disc → `placeWire`. Release anywhere else → stay armed, so click-click is unchanged.

Every finalize rule stands: fan-out, fan-in OR, one direct path, toggle-remove, **Cannot loop**, **Cannot wire here**.

## Flow channel

`VfxChannel = 'state' | 'burst' | 'flow'`.

`flow` is continuous: the Pixi ticker drives a transform, a tint, or a dash offset. No atlas frames, no per-instance sim state, no frame index anywhere.

Canon amendment on [[art/vfx]]: rigid-body transform of a sub-sprite is legal. Interpolating a raster frame is still banned. A marching dash and a turning part are transforms; a smeared bitmap is not.

`VFX_REDUCED`: `flow` paints its zero phase. Wet ground still paints. Bursts still do not mount.

`World.vfx` generalizes. It is a keyed state set any subsystem tick may write, not the sprinkler map. Each writer pings `'vfx'`. Key space this slice: `sprinkler:<v>`, `net:<id>`, `work:<seat>`. Nothing else.

## Pipe flow

`tickWater` writes `net:<id>` true iff that net moved water this tick. It already knows.

View, on net rebuild: BFS from that net's sources over conducting edges → per-segment `dist`. Dashes march low `dist` → high. Cycle `FLOW_DASH` — preference. A non-conducting edge does not march.

A closed valve stops the march on its edge. The topology explains itself, and every pipe and valve confusion above gets an answer that is not a tooltip.

Not lens-gated. Faint pipes march faint.

## Wire flow

High wire: stroke `water`, dashes march `from` → `to`, cycle `FLOW_DASH`. Low: solid `fruit-red`, no march. Paint stays `sensors` only.

## Wet ground

`plots` tints an owned cell from the soil `water` it already stores. All lenses. No new sim state, no new field, patched on `field`. `WET_TINT_MAX` — preference, alpha ramps on the water band.

The `water` lens stays the loud read. This is the quiet one, and it is what makes a sprinkler land somewhere instead of ending at its own sprite.

## Work progress

`dig` is a completion burst today — `finishWork` fires it after `doShovel` lands, so the animation plays on ground that is already tilled. It says *done*, and the player needed *doing*.

Delete `this.burst('dig', i.at)` from `finishWork`. `dig` becomes a **work state**, not a burst.

Sim adds nothing. `act.workLeft` and `act.workTotal` already carry the progress, the head `Intent` already carries `act` and `at`, and lockstep already replays both on every peer — the paint costs no traffic and needs no snapshot field.

`p = 1 - workLeft / workTotal`, view-derived. `workTotal === 0` is not a work state.

While the head intent is `shovel` and `workLeft > 0`:

- `vfx-dig` mounts at `at` as a `state` VFX, keyed `work:<seat>`. It starts when the spade does.
- The **destination** texture for that cell paints over it, masked to `p`. The dig visibly opens: a wipe across the cell, not a puff over it.

The masked sprite lives in `vfx` and unmounts the tick `doShovel` lands, when `plots` paints the tilled cell for real. One or the other, never both, no flash at the seam. Cancel or an interrupted queue drops the mask with no ground changed — nothing was written.

The keying is per seat, not per cell, so two gardeners digging is two masks and the mechanism is general: any timed act can claim `work:<seat>` by naming its asset at the arm site. This slice wires `shovel` only.

## Footprint outline

Hover paints one rect per occupied cell, so a hangar reads as six boxes with a grid drawn through it. It should read as one building.

One outline per **footprint**: the boundary of the union of its cells, internal edges dropped. Every footprint in the game is rectangular — 1×1, 2×1, 3×2, 2×3 — so that boundary is one rect today; spec it as the union boundary so an L-shaped building later does not reintroduce the grid.

Applies to both paths that stroke more than one cell:

- Unarmed hover of a multi-cell instance — house, hangar, field silo, still, pumpjack, rain-tank, tree, seed-silo, additive-store.
- Ghost footprints — `buy-pumpjack`, `buy-rain-tank`, `buy-still`, `buy-hangar`, the three field silos.

Still HTML overlay SVG over the canvas. `data-cell-stroke` moves onto the single outline; the *stroke the hover cell only* rule and the unhooked extra footprint rects both go — there is one element now. `stroke-ink` valid, `stroke-roof` blocked, `strokeWidth` 2, all unchanged.

Edge and vertex objects — pipe, valve, well, sprinkler — are not footprints and keep their own chrome. Drops and actors take no outline.

## Actor

`actor.svg` gains facing and a walk. Groups `s0`–`s3` per facing `s`, `n`, `e`. `w` is `e` mirrored on x. Twelve groups, one file, `0 0 24 24`. Hat group and the seat tint table hold per facing.

Facing from the seat's move delta, larger axis wins, `FACING_HYST` — preference — so it does not flicker on a diagonal. Standing paints `s0` of the last facing.

Walk is `flow`: cycle `WALK_DUR` — preference — phase per seat, advanced only while speed > 0.

Held-item anchor per facing, table on [[art/actor]].

Work swing is not in this slice.

## Cmd / Save

`Act.placeSmartValve` (`'I'`) is deleted; `'I'` frees. Delete `k: 'smart'` is deleted. The manual valve path — `confirmPlace` inside `click`, delete `k: 'pipe'` — covers both, and auto-lay lives in that body.

Save: `Gate` loses `'smart'`, `smartHold` becomes `valveHold`. Dump identity [[GLOBAL_VERSION]]. No migrate.

Digest: `valveHold` in place of `smartHold`.

Nothing here is logged that was not logged before. Lattice, routed preview, lens, lock, `toolLens`, port discs, flow, wet ground, facing, walk phase: view-local, none logged, none in Save, none in the digest.

## Unrepresentable

A gate that is smart. A valve that is closed because it is unwired. A locked lens with `lens === 'off'`. A `toolLens` with nothing armed. A pending run that is not a routed path. A port hit outside the painted disc. A partially paid run. A frame index in `World`. A work mask with `workTotal === 0`. A footprint outline carrying an internal edge.

## Landed

Shipped and graduated into the notes. Where the build differs from the spec above, the note is the contract and this is the record of why.

| spec | shipped |
|---|---|
| `World.vfx` key `work:<seat>` | the view reads the seat’s head intent and `workLeft` / `workTotal` directly, the way it already reads a machine’s working state. No sim write, no ping, no key. |
| `net:<id>` written by `tickWater` | not added. `Net` has no id, and minting one is a sim change this slice did not need. Pipe flow marches on wet, conducting segments — the grid is live — rather than only on the tick water actually moved. The closed-valve read, which is the payoff, is identical. |
| lattice while delete is armed | `PIPE_PLACE` skus only. Every empty edge lit up during a delete is noise: you delete pipe that exists. |
| click-click plants an anchor and places nothing | a click still places the one edge under it, then re-anchors at that edge’s far vertex so the next click continues the run. Chaining survives and the familiar single-click place is not taken away. |
| actor: 12 groups, facing `s` / `n` / `e`, `w` mirrored, 4-frame walk | walk **bob** only, on the flow channel, no new art. Facing and the side / back views are new pixel art and belong to a designer pass; the mechanism without the art would be a dead abstraction. |

Playtest on the built slice moved three lines, all in the notes:

- Pipe dashes paint only while the pipe overlay is up. Flow on faint pipe is motion on a network the player is not reading — [[architecture/view]] `view.flow`.
- The dig patch is ground: `VfxLayer.ground`, above `plots` and under the gardener, and the mask is a centred square opening outward, not a bar filling from the bottom — [[art/vfx]].
- An armed sprinkler snaps at `SPRINKLER_HIT` 0.45, not `VERTEX_HIT` 0.3: two thirds of a tile reaches a corner instead of a quarter. Placement does not compete with the cell under it; delete and inspect do, and keep the tight radius.

Two Playwright specs fail on `master` and still fail: `vehicles.spec` reads `[data-rake]` and `water.spec` reads `[data-weed]`, both DOM hooks the Pixi port removed. They predate this slice.

## Not this slice

Held for the next internal cut. Named so they are not re-derived.

Machine `flow` states: pumpjack walking beam, millstone turn and chute dust, still bubble and condenser drip, jam pot, well crank, rain-tank level, compost shimmer, tractor exhaust and dust trail. Pure fidelity, all of it. It reads better once the world is no longer confusing, which is what the rest of this note buys.
