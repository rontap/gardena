# Notices

Spec. Graduates into `docs/ui/notices.md` (new, link from [[ui/_index]]), [[ui/hud]], [[architecture/view]], [[architecture/modules]]. No `Save`. No `Cmd`. No `PROTOCOL`. No new `DirtyReason`. No sim change.

Copy: [[standards/user-facing-text]], [[standards/lexicon]].

Illegal in note bodies and in copy: **command center**, **infobox**, **alert**, **notification**, **problem**, **task**, **TODO**, **dashboard**. Borrowed from other games or from trades that name nothing here. One word carries this update — **notice**. `Notice` in `ui/multiplayer.tsx` is an unrelated identifier; the new files do not reuse that name.

The release name is the one exemption, on the user's call: the heading in `src/game/ui/changelog.md` reads **2.6.0 Command Center**. It is a proper name for one release and it names nothing in the game. Every line body under it, and every string, says **notice**.

## Preface

A farm now runs several clocks at once — a contract deadline, a research job, a plot going red, a tank draining, a tractor with no fuel — and the only way to find any of them is to look. The player who looks is doing the game's bookkeeping by hand; the player who does not, loses a crop and never learns why.

A notice is one line saying a clock is running out, or that something is waiting to be spent. It reads state and does nothing else.

The hard rule: **nothing flows back**. No notice writes to `World`, none is saved, none is a `Cmd`, none is digested. Delete the two files and the game is unchanged. That constraint is what makes the one-time rows below legal, and it is the thing to check any time this note is extended.

## What it reads

Every row is a pure read. `grow` covers six of them in one walk — and it already holds `dead` and `rotten`, which [[architecture/tick]] and [[architecture/world]] both fail to list. Fix those two tables.

| row | condition | reads | click |
|---|---|---|---|
| contract running | `contracts.active` entry | `dueDay - nowDay`, `sum(bin.filled) / need` | Market, Contracts tab |
| contract completed | one-time, below | `contracts.history` | Market, Contracts tab |
| no fuel | `fuel === 0` | `World.vehicles` | none |
| wilting / drowning | `waterBand(soil.water, tol) === 'red'`, `soil.drowning` splits the two | `grow` → `growing` | none |
| starving for fertilizer | `fertBand(soil.fertilizer, tol) === 'red'` | `grow` → `growing` | none |
| ready to harvest | `plant.freshness < FRESH_FULL` | `grow` → `ripe` | none |
| dead plant / rotten produce | that cell kind | `grow` | none |
| researching | `job.kind === 'run'` | `(seconds - left) / seconds` | Research dock |
| research done | one-time, below | `World.done` | Research dock |
| skill points | `World.points > 0` | — | Family |
| expansion permits | `World.expandLeft() > 0` | — | none |
| water network low | `stored / capacity < NOTICE_WATER_LOW` | `nets.grid(world)` | none |

Orange bands are not a notice. Only red. Orange is the warning the plot itself already paints; a second one in the corner would fire on most of a healthy farm.

`FRESH_FULL` — the cut inside `freshMul` ([`defs/crops.ts`](../../src/game/defs/crops.ts)) is an inline literal today. Name it there, tuned-to `freshMul`, and let both call sites read it. The notice and the sale multiplier must not be able to disagree about when fruit starts losing money.

`NOTICE_WATER_LOW` — preference. Per network, `stored` and `capacity` are the sums over `net.sources`. A network with no source is not low; it has no capacity to be low against, and it is skipped.

## Derivation

One pure function, `noticeRows(world): Notice[]`, in `src/game/ui/notices.ts`. It reads `World` and writes nothing. Not `sim/` — `sim/` is the game, and this is not.

It runs every `NOTICE_SECONDS` — preference, 1s. A timer, not a ping: the Hud only re-renders on `act` ([App.tsx:176](../../src/App.tsx:176)), and no ping fires at the moment a band goes red. Continuous world chrome does not get a `DirtyReason` — [[architecture/tick]].

Each `Notice` carries its own cells. That is what the map paints and what the column groups by.

### Two-pass listing

A condition enters the pending set on the first pass it holds, and becomes visible on the next pass it still holds. It leaves by the mirror rule. Visible delay is one to two passes, which is the point: a plot that dips red for a second while a sprinkler swings past is not a notice.

Pending and visible sets are React state in the hook. Not `World`.

### One-time rows

Contract completion and research completion are events, not conditions. Neither leaves a state to read: a contract that completes frees its slot on the delivering tick, and a research job that finishes goes idle and drops its id into `done`. Both are recovered by comparing the last pass to this one.

- The pass keeps the previous `active` ids. An id gone from `active` is looked up in `contracts.history` by `HistoryEntry.id`; `outcome.kind === 'done'` mints a completed row. `history` is a ring of `CONTRACT_HISTORY_MAX` and at most a handful close between two passes, so the id is still there.
- The pass keeps the previous `job`. A `run` on id `X` that is now not running, with `X` in `done`, mints a done row.

They skip the two-pass delay — delaying an event is wrong.

They clear when clicked, and on `dismissRecap()`. The recap already reports that day's contract outcomes and finished research ([[ui/docks]] Recap), so carrying them past it repeats a screen the player has just read. Not on the seam itself: play is frozen between the seam and `dismissRecap()`, and a row that clears while the recap is still up clears out of sight.

Cost of taking nothing from the sim: a completion that lands between the last pass and a reload or a `World` swap is never shown. Accepted. The alternative is a cursor in `Save`, and that is the thing this note refuses.

## Highlight

Hovering a row outlines every cell that row covers, on the map.

`MapView` takes one new prop — a `readonly Coord[]`, empty for none — and paints it through the existing `footOutline` into a fourth `<path>`, alongside `data-cell-stroke`, `data-furnace-cover` and `data-neighbour-reach` ([map.tsx:449](../../src/game/view/map.tsx:449)). Same shape, same stroke width, its own colour.

The map does not compute the set and does not learn the rule. Cells arrive from App, which holds the pass and the hovered row id. When the pass reruns while a row is hovered, the outline follows.

Scattered cells produce one path with disjoint subpaths off `footOutline`'s min-col / min-row origin. The `<svg>` is absolutely positioned and `overflow-visible`, so a farm-wide spread is one element. It is not N elements. Do not split it.

A vehicle is not a cell — it sits at a fractional pose in the actors layer. Its notice outlines the cell under `floor(x, y)`. A vehicle with no fuel is not moving, so that cell is stable.

Not a wash. A wash means a new parameter through `WorldView.patch` → `OverlayLayer.patch` ([overlay.ts:299](../../src/game/view/layers/overlay.ts:299)), which sits on the dirty path, to buy nothing the outline does not already give.

## Column

`Chrome` is not used: no background, no rails. The column is transparent and each row carries its own border.

`absolute top-20 right-4 z-20`, width one step under the bottom-right column's `w-80`, height capped so it ends around the middle of the page and never reaches Queue and Inspect at `right-4 bottom-4` ([App.tsx:961](../../src/App.tsx:961)).

Hidden while the vehicle editor is on — the Stops Window claims that exact anchor ([[ui/vehicles]]). Hidden while `seam.kind === 'recap'`.

### Row

```
{kind icon} {subject icon} {text}
{bar, when the row has a clock}
```

Kind icon is the notice: research, no fuel, wilting. Subject icon is the thing: the row being researched, the crop wilting, the good a contract still wants. A row whose subject is several things cycles them with `useCycle` at `CYCLE_MS` — the interval `AnyJamFace` already uses ([[ui/contracts]]). Do not mint a second one.

`animate-pulse` on change. One line, dense.

Rows of a kind group into one block, up to `NOTICE_GROUP_MAX` — preference, 3 — each with its own bar. Past that, one more line saying how many are not shown. That line is a plural string; it goes to [[agents/game-text-writer]] like the rest.

Bars are painted from `motion.ts` binds against the value the pass stamped, not from React every frame — same contract as the research bar in [[ui/hud]]. A bar between passes is interpolated from that stamp; it does not read `World`.

Dead plants and rotten produce have no bar. They do not worsen and they do not clear themselves, so there is no clock to draw. The row stands until the plot is dug.

### Controls

Hide is one small control on the column; hidden, the column slides off the right edge and leaves a tab to bring it back. App-local. Not a `Setting` — nothing behind the gear, nothing in `localStorage`.

No dismiss this update. Right-click and shift+right-click stay unbound here. Right-click already means cancel-place-or-drop on the map ([map.tsx:375](../../src/game/view/map.tsx:375)); the column sits above the canvas so a click on it never reaches that handler, but nothing in this update needs it.

## Multiplayer

A guest may not accept contracts, start research, pick a skill, or expand ([[mechanics/multiplayer]] `mp.guest`). Those four rows show for a guest and do not click through. Refill is guest-legal, so the fuel row is live for both.

## Invariants

To be stated on `docs/ui/notices.md` when it is written.

`notices.pure` — `noticeRows(world)` reads `World` and writes nothing. No notice is a `Cmd`, is saved, is digested, or sets a `DirtyReason`. Deleting `ui/notices.ts` and `ui/notices.tsx` leaves the sim unchanged.

`notices.pass` — The pass runs every `NOTICE_SECONDS` off a timer, never off a ping. A condition becomes visible on the second consecutive pass it holds and leaves on the second it does not.

`notices.once` — Contract and research completion are recovered by comparing the previous pass to this one, against `contracts.history` and `World.done`. They skip the two-pass delay and clear on click or on `dismissRecap()`. A completion spanning a reload or a `World` swap is not shown.

`notices.highlight` — `MapView` takes cells, never a rule. The hovered row's cells paint one `footOutline` path. Not a wash, not a `WorldView.patch` argument, not a Pixi change. A vehicle highlights the cell under `floor(x, y)`.

`notices.red` — Only a red band is a notice. Orange is not.

## Assumptions

`FRESH_FULL` is named in `defs/crops.ts` and read by both `freshMul` and the pass. One-time rows clear on `dismissRecap()` as well as on click. The column hides while the vehicle editor is on and while `seam.kind === 'recap'`. `grow` already carries `dead` and `rotten`; [[architecture/tick]] and [[architecture/world]] are corrected, not the index.
